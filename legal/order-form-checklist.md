# AI26 seller order — completion checklist

Use with draft v0.1 dated 16 October 2026. This checklist is not an executed order.

- Parties: Stoa Labs FZ-LLC trading as AI26; full Provider legal identity; incorporation/registration identifiers where needed; authorized signers.
- Private notice addresses and confirmed legal-notice emails for both parties.
- Effective date and fixed Terms version; attach a durable copy.
- Manifest ID, hash/version, sources, rights, date ranges, volumes, file formats, excluded categories, provenance evidence and any AI-generated material.
- Explicit prior-grant disclosures and any agreed exceptions; buyer or territory exclusivity, if any, with end date.
- Approved recipients/categories and AI purposes; explicit raw-data distribution permission if needed; source-identity disclosure consent if needed.
- Evaluation permission and access boundaries; acceptance and downstream-license activation sequence; no commercial training before the agreed trigger.
- Approved delivery method, deadline, batch definitions, support/documentation, cost budget and export responsibility.
- Review clock, rejection details, resubmission process, latent technical remedy and any negotiated departures.
- Fixed seller fee or precisely defined revenue share, currency, batch allocations, taxes, approved deductions, FX and payment charges.
- Explicit customer-funding condition, partial-payment rules, confirmed 15-calendar-day remittance deadline from AI26’s receipt of the relevant cleared customer payment (not invoice receipt), and long-stop/cancellation treatment if customer never pays.
- Privacy roles, lawful bases, required notices/consents, transfer countries and mandatory processing/transfer instruments; prohibited-data exceptions only where lawful.
- Security controls, incident contacts, two-business-day maximum notice subject to shorter law, recipient flow-down verification.
- Source-data retention/deletion date, backup cycle, legal holds, downstream deletion, derivative treatment and confirmation evidence.
- Liability/indemnity deviations and whether any negotiated cap actually covers customer-facing exposure; dispute and refund workflow.
- Governing law; competent court or arbitration seat, institution, rules, language, arbitrability, interim relief and mandatory-law exclusions.
- Signatures, dates, attachment list, document priority and preservation of a signed PDF/version archive.
