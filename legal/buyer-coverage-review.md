## Material decisions and dependencies

1. **Confirmed operator:** Stoa Labs FZ-LLC, Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates. Verified from the local company-letter source. No street address or assumed legal email published. Contract/service details still belong in signed orders.
2. **Confirmed pay-after-buyer model:** Seller fees are conditional on attributable cleared customer funds. User confirmed remittance within 15 calendar days after AI26 receives the relevant cleared customer payment. Invoice receipt does not extend that deadline. Specify partial receipts, bank charges, currency, withholding and any long-stop date. Website payout figures do not become guaranteed fees.
3. **License sequencing is intentionally different:** AI26’s own acceptance alone permits no production training. The commercial grant activates on acceptance plus customer cleared payment (or AI26 advance payment). The seller expressly permits the downstream grant before its own remittance. This must be reconciled with the buyer’s acquisition/payment language and any pre-payment access. Evaluation permissions must also be accepted downstream; an evaluation is not free training.
4. **Raw resale:** If AI26 must deliver the received corpus unchanged, explicit seller permission is necessary. A ban copied unchanged would undermine the intended business. Redaction alone is not a universal legal cure; define the actual prepared package and permission. Do not imply this seller form changes obligations owed under any buyer contract.
5. **Multiple customers:** The default permits multiple licensing tiers and customers, but a buyer order may require exclusivity or a no-prior-grant warranty. Check every transaction before promising another lab access. No blanket claim of free resale is made.
6. **Identity and privacy:** Public buyer confidentiality is not a promise to withhold information lawfully required by individuals, regulators or transfer instruments. Provider-identity nondisclosure also requires removing identifying content from the data itself. Obtain an express confidential-consent scope where due diligence requires source identification.
7. **Indemnity and caps:** A fee multiple based on AI26’s smaller seller payment may not cover its larger buyer exposure. The draft therefore leaves specified Provider rights/privacy/provenance/confidentiality claims uncapped and adds downstream claims. This is stronger than the source baseline and requires commercial and local-law review; it does not guarantee recovery or seller solvency. AI26’s own downstream wrongs remain its responsibility.
8. **Deletion and model rights:** Check actual export, acceptance, customer retention, backup and deletion workflows against 120 days and termination. Contractual permission cannot defeat mandatory deletion remedies. Derivatives cannot be a label for indefinitely retaining the original corpus.
9. **Jurisdiction remains open:** Do not treat Ras Al Khaimah as an implicit venue or copy California/JAMS by default. Select law, courts or seat, institution, rules, language and interim-relief treatment in the signed order. The draft addresses the subject but does not reproduce the source dispute arrangement.
10. **Missing buyer documents:** Coverage is limited to the public master terms. Obtain the proposed buyer order, manifest, exclusivity, audit and any privacy/transfer schedules. Some source obligations expressly live in those missing documents.
11. **Operational truth:** Encryption, two-business-day incident notice, recipient agreements, seven-year rights evidence and deletion are operational obligations. Validate the controls before agreeing; they are not fulfilled by publishing this page.
12. **Privacy notice still needed:** A commercial terms page is not a privacy notice. Replacing the footer link does not remove statutory notice, registration or data-broker duties. Have counsel assess actual collection and licensing in the UAE, India, Singapore, Germany/EU and any other affected jurisdictions. No country-specific compliance determination is made here.
